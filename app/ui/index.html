    Add SlothDJ to your browser One-time setup, about a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi.
    1. Added it with this guide before? Just reload it SlothDJ already put the new version in the folder. On your browser's extensions page (next step), click the reload ↻ icon on the SlothDJ card, or simply restart your browser. Then reload any open SoundCloud or YouTube tabs. That's all. Added it from a zip or another folder? Click Remove on that SlothDJ card instead, then follow the steps below.
    2. Open your browser's extensions page
      Or type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge). If an older SlothDJ is already listed there, click Remove on it first, so you don't end up with two.
    3. Turn on “Developer mode” It's a switch on that page: top right in Chrome and Brave, in the left panel in Edge.
    4. Click “Load unpacked” and pick the SlothDJ folder The folder's path is already copied. In the window that opens, click the address bar at the top, press Ctrl+V and Enter, then click Select Folder.
      …
    5. Pin it Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin SlothDJ. Then open any SoundCloud or YouTube page.
